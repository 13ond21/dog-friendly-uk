Dog-friendly days out in the UK & Ireland — quick answers

Are dogs allowed on UK beaches?

Most beaches allow dogs, but many have seasonal restrictions — typically dogs are banned on the main beach from around 1 May to 30 September. Our listings note where dogs are welcome all year, and it's always worth checking local signs before you go.

How do I find dog-friendly spots near a postcode?

Type a full or partial postcode (e.g. LA23, SW1, BT7, D13) into the search box to see listings in that area — or choose Closest to postcode to rank every spot by real distance.

What counts as a dog park?

Our Dog Parks category covers parks, commons, heaths, woods and estates with plenty of space for off-lead exercise — plus city parks and National Trust and Forestry estates where dogs are welcome.

How are listings chosen?

Every place is a real pub, beach, trail, dog park or public toilet we've researched, with an original description. Policies change, so we always recommend checking the official website (linked on every listing) before your visit.
